# Voice Change (Active ও Passive Voice)

**BCS English Grammar: মূল ধারণা, নিয়ম, উদাহরণ ও কঠিন প্রশ্ন অনুশীলন**

ব্যাখ্যা বাংলায়, উদাহরণ ইংরেজিতে। BCS প্রিলিমিনারি ও লিখিত, দুই পরীক্ষার জন্যই প্রযোজ্য।

## ১. Voice কী?

বাক্যের Verb দেখে বোঝা যায় কাজটি Subject নিজে করছে, নাকি কাজটি Subject-এর ওপর ঘটছে। একেই **Voice** বলে। Voice দুই প্রকার:

- **Active Voice:** Subject নিজে কাজ করে। জোর পড়ে কর্তার ওপর। উদাহরণ: *Rahim writes a letter.*
- **Passive Voice:** Subject কাজের ফল ভোগ করে। জোর পড়ে কর্মের ওপর। উদাহরণ: *A letter is written by Rahim.*

## ২. মূল ধারণা (Core Concepts)

- **Subject (কর্তা):** যে কাজ করে।
- **Object (কর্ম):** কাজটি যার ওপর ঘটে।
- **Agent:** Passive-এ 'by'-এর পরের অংশ (আগের Subject)।
- **Transitive Verb:** যে Verb-এর Object লাগে (eat, write, read, buy)। শুধু এদেরই Passive হয়।
- **Intransitive Verb:** Object লাগে না (go, come, sleep, die, arrive, fall)। এদের Passive **হয় না**।

**পরীক্ষার কৌশল:** Verb-এর পর 'কী?' বা 'কাকে?' প্রশ্ন করে উত্তর পেলে Verb Transitive।

### Passive কখন ব্যবহার করা হয়?

1. কর্তা অজানা বা গুরুত্বহীন হলে: *My bike was stolen.*
2. কাজটি বেশি গুরুত্বপূর্ণ হলে: *The bridge was built in 2010.*
3. Formal, scientific ও news writing-এ।

## ৩. Master Formula

**Active:** Subject + Verb + Object

**Passive:** Object (Subject-এর জায়গায়) + **be-form + V3** + by + Subject (Object-এর form-এ)

উদাহরণ: *She (S) eats (V) rice (O).* → *Rice is eaten by her.*

## ৪. Tense অনুযায়ী Passive ছক

| Tense | Passive Structure | Example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Present Indefinite | am/is/are + V3 | He writes a letter. → A letter is written by him. |
| Present Continuous | am/is/are + **being** + V3 | She is cooking rice. → Rice is being cooked by her. |
| Present Perfect | has/have + **been** + V3 | They have finished the work. → The work has been finished by them. |
| Past Indefinite | was/were + V3 | He wrote a letter. → A letter was written by him. |
| Past Continuous | was/were + **being** + V3 | They were playing football. → Football was being played by them. |
| Past Perfect | had + **been** + V3 | She had completed the work. → The work had been completed by her. |
| Future Indefinite | will/shall + **be** + V3 | I shall do the work. → The work will be done by me. |
| Future Perfect | will/shall have + **been** + V3 | He will have finished it. → It will have been finished by him. |

**মনে রাখো:** Present, Past ও Future-এর Perfect Continuous এবং Future Continuous Tense-এর সাধারণত Passive হয় না।

**Memory trick:** Continuous হলে **being**, Perfect হলে **been**, Future হলে **be**।

## ৫. Pronoun-এর পরিবর্তন

| Subject form | Object form |
| --- | --- |
| I | me |
| We | us |
| You | you |
| He | him |
| She | her |
| They | them |
| It | it |
| Who | whom (by whom) |
| Whom | who |

Noun-এর form বদলায় না। Passive থেকে Active-এ গেলে উল্টো নিয়ম খাটে (me → I, by whom → who)।

## ৬. ধাপে ধাপে পদ্ধতি

1. Subject, Verb ও Object চিহ্নিত করো।
2. Tense চিনে নাও।
3. Object-কে Subject-এর জায়গায় বসাও (প্রয়োজনে Pronoun-এর form বদলাও)।
4. Tense অনুযায়ী be-form বসিয়ে Verb-কে V3 করো।
5. 'by' + আগের Subject (Object form-এ) শেষে বসাও।
6. Adverb ও বাকি শব্দ নিজের জায়গায় রাখো।

**উদাহরণ:** *The teacher is teaching us grammar every day.*

- Subject = The teacher, Verb = is teaching (Present Continuous), Objects = us, grammar
- Passive: *We are being taught grammar by the teacher every day.*

## ৭. বাক্যের ধরন অনুযায়ী নিয়ম

### ক. Negative Sentence

Do not / does not / did not থাকলে Passive-এ do-form বাদ যায়, 'not' বসে be-form-এর পর।

- She does not eat rice. → Rice is not eaten by her.
- I did not do it. → It was not done by me.
- He never tells a lie. → A lie is never told by him.

**No one / Nobody / None** Subject হলে Passive-এ 'not ... by anyone' হয়:

- Nobody can solve this problem. → This problem cannot be solved by anyone.
- No one has seen him. → He has not been seen by anyone.

### খ. Interrogative Sentence

**(i) Auxiliary দিয়ে শুরু (Do/Does/Did/Is/Has/Will/Can):** Auxiliary + Object + be-form + V3 + by Subject?

- Did you see the film? → Was the film seen by you?
- Has he done the work? → Has the work been done by him?
- Can you do it? → Can it be done by you?
- Do you know him? → Is he known to you?

**(ii) Wh-word দিয়ে শুরু:**

- **Who** (Subject) থাকলে → **By whom**: *Who wrote this poem?* → *By whom was this poem written?*
- **Whom** (Object) থাকলে → **Who**: *Whom did you meet?* → *Who was met by you?*
- *What do you want?* → *What is wanted by you?*
- *Why did you break the glass?* → *Why was the glass broken by you?*
- *Who is writing the letter?* → *By whom is the letter being written?*

### গ. Imperative Sentence (আদেশ, অনুরোধ, উপদেশ)

- **Affirmative:** Let + Object + be + V3
  - Open the door. → Let the door be opened.
  - Do it at once. → Let it be done at once.
- **Negative:** Let not + Object + be + V3 (অথবা Object + should not be + V3)
  - Do not tell a lie. → Let not a lie be told.
  - Never tell a lie. → A lie should never be told.
- **Request (Please/Kindly):** You are requested to ...
  - Please help me. → You are requested to help me.
- **Let-sentence:** *Let him do it.* → *Let it be done by him.*

### ঘ. Modal Verb

সূত্র: **Modal + be + V3**

- He can do the work. → The work can be done by him.
- You must obey the rules. → The rules must be obeyed by you.
- We should respect teachers. → Teachers should be respected by us.

**Semi-modal:**

- have to / has to / had to → **has to be / have to be / had to be + V3**: *I have to finish the work.* → *The work has to be finished by me.*
- is going to → **is going to be + V3**: *She is going to buy a car.* → *A car is going to be bought by her.*
- used to / ought to → **used to be / ought to be + V3**: *Students ought to obey teachers.* → *Teachers ought to be obeyed by students.*

### ঙ. দুটি Object-যুক্ত বাক্য (Direct ও Indirect Object)

Indirect Object (ব্যক্তি) ও Direct Object (বস্তু), যেকোনোটিকে Subject করা যায়। ব্যক্তিকে Subject করা বেশি প্রচলিত।

- He gave me a book. → **I was given a book by him.** / A book was given to me by him.
- The teacher taught us grammar. → We were taught grammar by the teacher. / Grammar was taught to us by the teacher.
- He bought me a pen. → A pen was bought for me by him. (buy, make, get-এর সাথে for বসে)

### চ. Preposition-যুক্ত Verb ও Phrasal Verb

Preposition Verb-এর সাথেই থাকে, বাদ দেওয়া যাবে না।

- They laughed at the beggar. → The beggar was laughed at by them.
- He looked after the child. → The child was looked after by him.
- The police are looking into the matter. → The matter is being looked into by the police.
- The car ran over the dog. → The dog was run over by the car.
- We must take care of the poor. → The poor must be taken care of by us.

অন্য গুরুত্বপূর্ণ Phrasal Verb: *send for, wait for, listen to, speak to, put off, call off, turn down, look up to, run after*।

### ছ. Perception ও Causative Verb (see, hear, watch, make, let)

Active-এ Object-এর পর **bare infinitive** (to ছাড়া Verb) থাকলে Passive-এ **to** বসে।

- I saw him go. → He was seen **to go** by me.
- I heard her sing. → She was heard **to sing** by me.
- They made him work hard. → He was made **to work** hard by them.
- They let him go. → He was **allowed to go**. (let-এর Passive হয় allowed to)

**ব্যতিক্রম:** Verb-ing থাকলে to বসে না: *I saw him going.* → *He was seen going by me.*

### জ. Impersonal Passive ('that' clause)

People say / They believe / Everybody knows + that-clause থাকলে দুটি রূপ সম্ভব:

- People say that honesty is the best policy. → **It is said that** honesty is the best policy.
- They say that he is rich. → It is said that he is rich. / **He is said to be rich.**
- They believe that he is honest. → It is believed that he is honest. / He is believed to be honest.
- People expect that he will win. → It is expected that he will win. / He is expected to win.

### ঝ. Agent (by...) কখন বাদ যায়?

অনির্দিষ্ট কর্তা (they, people, someone, somebody, we, one, everybody) থাকলে Passive-এ সাধারণত **by-অংশ বাদ** যায়।

- They speak English in America. → English is spoken in America.
- Somebody has stolen my purse. → My purse has been stolen.
- Someone is knocking at the door. → The door is being knocked at.

কর্তা নির্দিষ্ট ও গুরুত্বপূর্ণ হলে (by Rahim, by the police) রাখতে হয়।

### ঞ. 'by'-এর বদলে অন্য Preposition (BCS-এর প্রিয় টপিক)

কিছু Verb-এর Passive-এ by বসে না। এগুলো মুখস্থ রাখো:

| Active | Passive |
| --- | --- |
| Everybody knows him. | He is **known to** everybody. |
| The news surprised me. | I was **surprised at** the news. |
| The result pleased my father. | My father was **pleased with** the result. |
| His behaviour annoyed me. | I was **annoyed at/with** his behaviour. |
| This subject interests me. | I am **interested in** this subject. |
| The result satisfied them. | They were **satisfied with** the result. |
| Smoke filled the room. | The room was **filled with** smoke. |
| Dust covered the table. | The table was **covered with** dust. |
| The story bored us. | We were **bored with** the story. |
| The accident shocked everyone. | Everyone was **shocked at** the accident. |
| The noise frightened the child. | The child was **frightened at/of** the noise. |
| Rahim married Karima. | Karima was **married to** Rahim. |
| The failure disappointed him. | He was **disappointed at/with** the failure. |

আরও মনে রাখো: be made **of** (উপাদান চেনা যায়: table is made of wood), be made **from** (উপাদান চেনা যায় না: paper is made from wood), be tired **of/with**, be worried **about**, be crowded **with**।

### ট. যে Verb-এর Passive হয় না

- **Intransitive Verb:** go, come, sleep, die, arrive, fall, laugh, stand। *He goes to school.* (Passive নেই)
- **Stative (অবস্থাবাচক) Transitive Verb:** have (মালিকানা অর্থে), lack, resemble, suit, fit, become (মানানো অর্থে), cost, weigh, equal।
  - He has a car. (Passive নেই)
  - She resembles her mother. (Passive নেই)
  - The dress suits you. (Passive নেই)

### ঠ. Passive থেকে Active

নিয়ম: by-এর পরের অংশ Subject হয়, be-form বাদ যায়, V3 Tense অনুযায়ী বদলায়, Pronoun-এর case ঠিক করতে হয়। by না থাকলে **They / People / Someone / We / One** বসাও।

- The work was done by him. → He did the work.
- By whom was the thief caught? → Who caught the thief?
- The book has been bought. → Someone has bought the book.
- English is spoken all over the world. → People speak English all over the world.
- Let the door be shut. → Shut the door.
- He is known to me. → I know him.

### ড. Compound, Complex ও বিশেষ বাক্য

**Compound/Complex বাক্যে প্রতিটি clause আলাদাভাবে পরিবর্তন করতে হয়,** তবে Intransitive Verb-যুক্ত clause অপরিবর্তিত থাকে।

- He opened the door and entered the room. → The door was opened by him and the room was entered by him.
- He went home and wrote a letter. → He went home and a letter was written by him.
- If you do the work, you will get a reward. → If the work is done by you, you will be rewarded.
- While I was reading the book, he wrote a letter. → While the book was being read by me, a letter was written by him.

**Exclamatory:** What a nice picture he has drawn! → What a nice picture has been drawn by him!

**Time adverb:** He teaches us English every day. → We are taught English by him every day.

## ৮. সাধারণ ভুল (Common Mistakes)

1. **being / been গুলিয়ে ফেলা:** The letter has *being* written. ❌ → has *been* written ✅
2. **ভুল V3:** Rice is *ate* by him. ❌ → *eaten* ✅
3. **Pronoun-এর case:** written by *he*. ❌ → by *him* ✅
4. **Intransitive Verb-এর Passive:** The book was *belonged* to me. ❌ (belong Intransitive)
5. **Word order:** By whom the work was done? ❌ → By whom *was the work* done? ✅
6. **Modal-এ be বাদ:** The work can done by him. ❌ → can *be* done ✅
7. **Perception verb-এ to ভোলা:** He was seen go by me. ❌ → was seen *to go* ✅
8. **'known by' ভুল ব্যবহার:** He is known *by* everybody. ❌ → known *to* everybody ✅

## ৯. দ্রুত Revision Tips

- প্রথমে Tense চিনো, তারপর be-form বসাও।
- Continuous = **being**, Perfect = **been**, Future = **be**।
- Imperative = **Let + O + be + V3**।
- who → **by whom**, whom → **who**।
- see/hear/make + bare infinitive → Passive-এ **to** বসে।
- know → **known to**, surprise → **surprised at**, please → **pleased with**।
- Intransitive Verb ও have (মালিকানা)-এর Passive নেই।
- কর্তা অনির্দিষ্ট হলে (people, someone, they) **by-অংশ বাদ** দাও।

## ১০. পরীক্ষামুখী কঠিন প্রশ্ন অনুশীলন

নিচের প্রশ্নগুলো BCS-এর মান ও ধরন অনুযায়ী সাজানো। আগে নিজে সমাধান করো, তারপর শেষের উত্তরমালা মিলিয়ে নাও।

### Section A: MCQ (Change the voice)

**1.** Who taught you English?

(a) Who was taught English by you? (b) By whom were you taught English? (c) Whom were you taught English by? (d) By whom English was taught you?

**2.** Let him do the work.

(a) Let the work done by him. (b) Let the work be done by him. (c) The work is let to be done by him. (d) Let the work was done by him.

**3.** They made him sign the paper.

(a) He was made to sign the paper by them. (b) He was made sign the paper by them. (c) He made to sign the paper by them. (d) The paper was made to sign by him.

**4.** The news surprised everybody.

(a) Everybody was surprised with the news. (b) Everybody was surprised at the news. (c) Everybody was surprising at the news. (d) Everybody had been surprised at the news.

**5.** Nobody can solve the problem.

(a) The problem can be solved by anybody. (b) The problem cannot be solved by anybody. (c) The problem is not solved by anyone. (d) The problem cannot solve by anybody.

**6.** Please help me.

(a) You are requested to help me. (b) I am requested to help. (c) Me is requested to be helped. (d) Let me is helped.

**7.** Everybody knows him.

(a) He is known by everybody. (b) He is known to everybody. (c) He knows everybody. (d) He has known to everybody.

**8.** Has the postman delivered the letter?

(a) Has the letter delivered by the postman? (b) Has the letter been delivered by the postman? (c) Had the letter been delivered by the postman? (d) Is the letter delivered by the postman?

**9.** He is going to buy a car.

(a) A car is going to be bought by him. (b) A car is being going to buy by him. (c) A car will be bought by him. (d) A car is gone to be bought by him.

**10.** I saw him going.

(a) He was seen to go by me. (b) He was seen going by me. (c) He was seen to going by me. (d) He saw me going.

**11.** People say that he is honest.

(a) It is said that he is honest. (b) He was said that honest. (c) It said that he is honest. (d) He is said that honest.

**12.** Which sentence CANNOT be changed into passive voice?

(a) She has a beautiful voice. (b) She sings a song. (c) She wrote a letter. (d) She is reading a book.

**13.** Who is writing the letter?

(a) By whom is the letter written? (b) By whom is the letter being written? (c) Who the letter is being written by? (d) By whom was the letter being written?

**14.** Whom did you meet?

(a) Who was met by you? (b) Whom was met by you? (c) By whom were you met? (d) Who did you meet?

**15.** We must take care of the poor.

(a) The poor must be taken care of by us. (b) The poor must be taken care by us. (c) The poor must take care of us. (d) The poor must be taking care of by us.

**16.** Which is the active form of 'The boy was run over by a car'?

(a) A car ran over the boy. (b) A car runs over the boy. (c) A car had run over the boy. (d) The boy ran over a car.

**17.** Do not tell a lie.

(a) Let not a lie be told. (b) A lie not be told. (c) A lie did not be told. (d) A lie is not told.

**18.** He gave me a book. Which passive is correct?

(a) I was given a book by him. (b) I was given to a book by him. (c) I gave a book by him. (d) A book gave me by him.

**19.** What a nice picture he has drawn!

(a) What a nice picture has been drawn by him! (b) How nice a picture drawn by him! (c) What a nice picture was drawn by him! (d) What a nice picture is drawn by him!

**20.** They will have completed the project by next year.

(a) The project will have been completed by them by next year. (b) The project will be completed by them by next year. (c) The project would have been completed by them by next year. (d) The project will have completed by them by next year.

## ১২. পরীক্ষার জন্য শেষ কথা

BCS-এ Voice Change সাধারণত Imperative, Interrogative (Wh-question), Modal, Phrasal Verb, Perception Verb ও 'know/surprise/please'-জাতীয় Preposition-এর ওপর কঠিন প্রশ্ন আসে। প্রতিদিন ১০টি বাক্য নিজে Active থেকে Passive ও Passive থেকে Active করলে ১ সপ্তাহেই এই টপিকে দখল তৈরি হবে। MCQ-তে সময় বাঁচাতে প্রথমে Tense চিনে নিয়ে সঠিক be-form দিয়ে অপশন বাদ দাও, এরপর 'to / by / at / with'-এর মতো Preposition মেলাও।
